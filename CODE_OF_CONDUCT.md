# Code of conduct

This project uses the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
including its enforcement guidelines. The link is the canonical text; it is not
copied here so there is only one version to keep current.

What it comes down to:

- Be critical of the work and civil to the person. A blunt review of a diff is
  fine. Contempt for whoever wrote it is not.
- Assume the other person is working around a constraint you cannot see. Most
  disagreements here are about one nobody has written down yet.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and deliberate disruption are not
  welcome in issues, pull requests, commit messages, or anywhere the project is
  the subject.
- Do not post other people's private information, or material the project has no
  right to distribute.

## Scope

Project spaces, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents to the maintainer through [GitHub](https://github.com/Hyper-Dragon):
a direct message, or an address from the profile. Do not open a public issue.

The maintainer reads reports alone. Expect an acknowledgement within a week and a
reply explaining what happens next. The Contributor Covenant's enforcement
guidelines describe the possible responses, from a private warning to a permanent
ban.

Retaliation against someone who reports an incident is itself a violation.

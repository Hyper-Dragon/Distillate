# Code of conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The canonical text — including the enforcement guidelines —
lives at that link and is incorporated here by reference rather than copied, so
there is only one version of it to keep current.

In short, and without replacing the text at that link:

- Be direct about the work and civil about the person. A terse review of a diff is
  fine; contempt for the person who wrote it is not.
- Assume the other person is trying to solve a problem you cannot see yet. Most
  disagreements here are about a constraint nobody has written down.
- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sustained disruption are not
  welcome, in issues, pull requests, commit messages or anywhere else the project
  is the subject.
- Do not post other people's private information, or content the project has no
  right to distribute.

## Scope

Project spaces, and any space where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report incidents privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Hyper-Dragon) — a direct message, or an email address
from the maintainer's profile. Do not open a public issue.

Reports are read by the maintainer alone. Expect an acknowledgement within a week,
and an answer about what happens next. The enforcement guidelines in the Contributor
Covenant describe the range of responses, from a private warning to a permanent ban.

Retaliation against someone who reports an incident is itself a violation.
